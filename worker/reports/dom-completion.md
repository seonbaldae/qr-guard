# DOM analyzer handoff

Copy `worker/dom-analyzer.cjs` and `worker/tests/dom-analyzer.test.cjs` into the Worker repository. No Worker integration is performed here. Call `createDomAnalyzer(page, options)` after rendering; `collect()` performs inspection, `result()`, `observations()`, and `limitations()` return independent snapshots, and `stop()` is idempotent. Callers must retain limitations: a positive password count during incomplete inspection is a lower bound, not a complete count. An untrustworthy zero or total inspection failure returns null. Form count in `dom_inspection.observedForms` is the number encountered within visited nodes, not necessarily the total; `incomplete` and limitations qualify it. `sensitiveFieldTypes` uses `password`, `otp`, and `payment_card`.

`officialDomain` must be a prevalidated registered domain supplied by its owner; `resolveRegisteredDomain(host)` must be supplied by the integrator and use a PSL-aware implementation. No mapping or resolver is included. Form-action comparison records only mismatch facts and does not mean submission or exfiltration. Limitations always note closed shadow roots and unknown comparison when appropriate. Frame failures and caps are reported without frame URLs or exception text. Titles are bounded and heuristically redact obvious URLs/token assignments; arbitrary sensitive prose in titles cannot be guaranteed detectable. DOM is a point-in-time snapshot, not proof of safety or future behavior. No user input, clicking, navigation, requests, or submissions are initiated by the analyzer.

The node:test suite uses controlled mock frame/DOM objects, not Playwright or real browser integration. It checks the callback's traversal and aggregation, failure and cap semantics, secret sentinel exclusion, comparison, and snapshots. Real Chromium/Playwright rendering, cross-origin frames and Docker integration remain for the parent to test under the prescribed network isolation.
